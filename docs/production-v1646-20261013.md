## Behavior

- Duplicate candidates require matching normalized names (ignoring trailing
  `copy`/`копия` and copy numbers), weight, dimensions, color, categories,
  plain-text notes, photo references, availability and nesting capability.
  A lone record named `copy` is no longer a candidate.
- Different photo references or weights separate same-name tents/socks.
  Photos are compared by stored references, not visual image similarity;
  independently uploaded or offline-cloned photos with different references
  may therefore prevent an otherwise matching pair from appearing.
- Broken items are excluded from duplicate groups and have a separate
  `Сломанные (N)` option in the Items participation selector.
- Items and Bags render matching records together in numbered groups. Groups
  of three or more contain every matching record, rather than repeated pairs.
  `Дубликаты (N)` counts records including originals, not groups or excess copies.
  Search retains the companions of a matching record for comparison.
- Nothing is deleted or merged automatically.
